Critique and recommendation
  • Recommended: Pip. The little screen-head belongs beside a command line, the continuous outline survives at normal size, and its expression feels warm without demanding attention.
  • Alternatives: Twig is the quieter, more observant choice. Spark is sharper and more energetic.
  • Measured geometry: Pip has a 9-column head with a blank cell between each eye and mouth, inside a 12-column strip. The other concepts use 9-column sprite rows.
  • Motion: The preview changes only eyes or mouth. Reduced-motion disables it entirely.
