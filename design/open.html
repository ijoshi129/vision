01 / Steal the card, not the lecture

        
Codex is five rows, left-aligned, labeled. Claude’s boxed welcome adds tips and recent activity and eats a third of the screen. That was the right no. The card is the yes.

      

      

        
02 / Pip is the logo

        
Idle face on the left of the card, same glyphs as the gutter. On open he is how you know you sat down. Once you type, he is still in the composer doing the real job.

      

      

        
03 / Voice is the extra row

        
They have model and directory. We have those plus whether it’s going to talk. Mode and wake stay on the status row; they already live there. Don’t duplicate them.

      

      

        
04 / It scrolls like theirs

        
Not a splash. First block in an empty transcript; /clear puts it back. A resumed thread shows the history instead. Talk and serve use the same card with their extra lines under it.
